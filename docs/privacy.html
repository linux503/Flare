Flare Pro 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

Flare Pro（以下简称“本应用”）是一款在本机运行的截图、录屏与标注工具。我们重视你的隐私，本应用不需要注册登录，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人数据。

1. 本地处理

截图、录屏、长截图、标注、隐私脱敏和文字识别（OCR）全部在你的 Mac 上完成。生成的文件只保存在你选择的位置（默认“图片/Flare”），不会发送到我们或任何第三方的服务器。

2. 系统权限

  • 屏幕录制：用于截图和录屏，画面只在本机处理。
  • 麦克风（可选）：仅在你开启“录制人声”时使用，音频只写入本地录屏文件。
  • 辅助功能（可选）：仅用于长截图时自动滚动页面。

你可以随时在“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性”中关闭上述权限。

3. 网络访问

只有在你主动使用“网页证据快照”时，本应用才会访问你输入的网址以抓取页面与证书信息；若网址是区块链交易页面，还可能向公共区块链节点（如 publicnode.com、mempool.space）查询该交易。这些请求不携带任何能识别你身份的信息，抓取结果只保存在本机。

4. 数据收集与第三方

本应用不包含广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK，不追踪你的使用行为。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

6. 政策变更与联系方式

如本政策有更新，会在本页面公布。如有疑问，请通过 GitHub Issues 联系我们。

Flare Pro Privacy Policy

Effective: November 4, 2026

Flare Pro is an on-device screenshot, screen recording and annotation tool. It requires no account and does not collect, upload or sell any personal data.

1. On-device processing

Screenshots, recordings, scrolling captures, annotation, redaction and OCR all run locally on your Mac. Output files are saved only to the location you choose (default: Pictures/Flare) and are never sent to us or any third party.

2. System permissions

  • Screen Recording: to capture screenshots and recordings, processed locally.
  • Microphone (optional): used only when you enable voice recording; audio is written to the local recording file only.
  • Accessibility (optional): used only to auto-scroll content during scrolling captures.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Network access

The app connects to the network only when you use “Web Evidence Snapshot”: it fetches the URL you enter and its certificate details. For blockchain transaction pages it may also query public nodes (e.g. publicnode.com, mempool.space) for that transaction. No identifying information is sent, and results are stored locally.

4. Data collection and third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and does not track usage.

5. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: GitHub Issues.
